Find a team for TFP shoots.

TFP (Time for Print) is a way for models and photographers to collaborate on photos for their portfolios. Agree on the shoot's concept, terms, and use of the photos with your team beforehand.

Use the TFP Models mobile app to browse creator profiles, find models and photographers, explore castings, and discuss shoots in chat. Download it for iPhone or follow our Android installation guide.
