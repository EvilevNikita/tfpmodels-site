Account deletion

Delete your TFP Models account and associated data

Request deletion by email

  1. Email support@tfpmodels.org with the subject Privacy Request - Delete account.
  2. State that you want to delete your TFP Models account and its associated data. Include your profile name, the email linked to the account, and whether you sign in with email, Google, or Apple. You do not need to reinstall the app to send a request.
  3. Never send your password, sign-in codes, or recovery codes.

A TFP Models administrator handles email requests manually. To verify ownership, the administrator sends a confirmation to the email linked to your account; reply to that message to confirm the request. A profile name or public profile information alone is not proof of ownership. If you use Apple Hide My Email, mention this so the administrator can use your linked relay address. If you cannot receive messages at your linked address, explain this in your request so an alternative verification method can be arranged before any deletion.

The administrator confirms the deletion schedule and cancellation instructions after verifying ownership. Sending an email does not automatically start the in-app 7-day timer. There is currently no fixed email-response deadline; do not assume your account has been deleted until the administrator confirms completion.

Delete from the Android or iOS app

  1. Open your profile, then Settings, account management, and Delete account. Labels may vary with the app language.
  2. Follow the on-screen identity confirmation and confirm the deletion request.
  3. The app schedules deletion for 7 days after the request. Signing back in before the deletion is carried out cancels the pending request.

The server checks for due requests every hour. The scheduled date is not a guarantee that every copy in every system disappears at that exact moment. The 7-day delay and sign-in cancellation above describe requests made in the app.

What account deletion removes

Account deletion removes your TFP Models sign-in account and profile, including contact and profile information; uploaded profile and casting-reference images; castings you created; casting applications linked to you as applicant or author; conversations you participate in and their messages; and associated in-app support requests, user reports, blocks, agreement records, and device registrations stored under your profile.

Completed account deletion cannot be undone. Deleting your TFP Models account does not delete your separate Google or Apple account.

See data retention and copies below and our Privacy Policy.

Keep your account

Delete specific data without deleting your account

Remove a gallery photo in the app

Open your own profile in the Android or iOS app, select a gallery photo, and choose the delete-photo action. This removes that photo while keeping your account. The profile avatar has a separate replacement action.

Request specific data deletion by email

  1. Email support@tfpmodels.org with the subject Privacy Request - Delete specific data.
  2. Include your profile name, the email linked to the account, and your sign-in method. State Keep my account and identify the specific items you want removed. For a photo, describe which gallery image it is; do not send unrelated private content.
  3. Never send your password, sign-in codes, or recovery codes.

The administrator can remove your identified profile photos while keeping your account. Ownership is checked using the email-confirmation process above. Photo removal is carried out when the verified request is processed; sending the email does not remove the photo automatically. There is currently no fixed email-response deadline.

Update your profile information

You can edit your profile in the app, replace information, or clear optional fields and save. The saved changes update your current profile without deleting your account. Required fields cannot be left empty. This does not mean that older information has been erased from every log, backup, or copy already received by another person.

What happens when you delete a chat

The delete-chat action hides the conversation from the active chat list; it does not erase its messages from the server. Chat history is retained for moderation and investigating abusive behavior. There is currently no automatic 7-day purge or fixed retention period for these hidden chats. The photo-deletion service above does not include permanent deletion of individual chat messages. Account deletion removes the associated conversations and messages as described above.

Data retention

Retained data and other copies

The account-deletion process removes the account records and uploaded images described above from the app's active Firebase database, authentication service, and storage. It does not itself erase support emails, analytics records, service logs, or provider backups.

Support emails are handled separately from in-app support requests and are not automatically erased by account deletion. A fixed retention period for support email has not yet been established. If your request includes correspondence with support, identify it in your email.

Google Analytics for Firebase processes usage events separately from the account database. Account deletion does not currently trigger deletion of those analytics records. The exact configured retention periods for analytics, service logs, and provider backup copies have not yet been verified; we cannot promise that they are erased within 7 days. See Firebase privacy information and Google Analytics retention information for the providers' controls and policies.

For a request involving these records, contact the administrator at support@tfpmodels.org to identify the data and discuss the applicable deletion options. Copies independently saved by other people, such as screenshots, are outside this account-deletion process.
