Community Rules

Version 2026-10-26.1 — TFP Models

Effective October 26, 2026. This version replaces the previous published version (2026-10-26). Nudity is not permitted in TFP Models. There is no exception for artistic photographs or adult viewers.

These rules apply to profiles, photos, castings, links and messages in TFP Models. Before creating or uploading content, you must accept the current version of the rules.

Only post content you have the right to share. Obtain permission from the people depicted; do not impersonate others or misrepresent your identity, age or casting conditions.

Do not post nude photographs or other images showing genitals, the anus, exposed buttocks or visible nipples of female breasts. Pornography, sexually explicit poses or sexual acts, sexual service offers and content that sexualizes minors are prohibited. Calling an image artistic, hiding it or labeling it as adult content does not make it acceptable. A bare male chest alone is not prohibited. Swimwear or lingerie alone is not prohibited, provided the image meets these rules.

Art Nude may be selected as a professional specialization. It describes the work you do outside the service and does not permit you to publish nude photographs in TFP Models.

Child sexual abuse and exploitation, grooming, trafficking and solicitation of minors are strictly prohibited. Do not request or share such material.

Threats, harassment, bullying, hate speech, violence, illegal activities, fraud, spam and disclosure of private information without permission are prohibited. Respect refusals and personal boundaries. Castings must clearly describe the work and any payment; do not use them to lure people into unsafe or deceptive situations.

Use the report action on a profile or content to notify moderators; select the reason and identify the relevant content. Blocking a user restricts interaction. You can also contact support@tfpmodels.org about safety concerns. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Reports are reviewed by the TFP Models team. Violating content may be removed or hidden and accounts may be restricted or blocked. Contact support@tfpmodels.org to request review of a moderation decision. Do not submit deliberately false reports. Report snapshots may be retained while the report is reviewed, subject to our Privacy Policy.

Acceptance applies to a specific version of these rules. Material changes require renewed acceptance before creating or uploading further content. Acceptance is not blanket consent to all processing of personal data. These rules do not replace the service's existing age requirements or the Privacy Policy.

Child safety contact

For child safety concerns in TFP Models, contact the TFP Models team at support@tfpmodels.org. You can also report concerns using the reporting feature within the app.
