Community Rules

Version 2026-10-26 — TFP Models

This version takes effect when the sensitive-photo feature is released. Until then, the previous rules (2026-09-06) remain in force.

The label “Sensitive photo” describes a content category, not a legal age classification. Separately, TFP Models limits viewing other users’ labeled photos and applying labels to accounts whose supplied date of birth indicates an age of at least 18. Store ratings are determined separately.

These rules apply to profiles, photos, castings, links and messages in TFP Models. Before creating or uploading content, you must accept the current version of the rules.

Only post content you have the right to share. Obtain permission from the people depicted; do not impersonate others or misrepresent your identity, age or casting conditions. The account author's age does not establish the age of the people depicted.

Contextual artistic photographs depicting adult nudity may be included in a portfolio if they comply with all other rules and are marked as sensitive before publication. Only users aged 18 or older may publish this category, regardless of their role, including photographers. Mark artistic nudes, exposed buttocks and visible nipples of female breasts as sensitive. A bare male chest alone does not require this label. Lingerie or boudoir photographs without an explicit pose do not require it solely on that basis either. Moderators assess borderline poses and context; calling an image “art” does not override the prohibitions.

Pornography, sexually explicit content, depictions of genitals or the anus, sexual service offers and content that sexualizes children are prohibited. Child sexual abuse and exploitation, grooming, trafficking and solicitation of minors are strictly prohibited. Do not request or share such material. A sensitive-content label or a viewer's confirmation does not make prohibited material acceptable.

The supported app hides photos marked as sensitive from ordinary viewers by default. An adult viewer confirms viewing for the current profile visit. Users aged 13–17 can still access profiles but cannot reveal photos labeled as sensitive within the app. Revealing is also unavailable when age is unknown or invalid. Owners see their own photos without confirmation; moderators reveal other people's photos using the adult-viewer confirmation.

Manually label applicable photos before publication. Selecting a specialization, including artNude, does not label photos automatically. After saving, the app does not let the owner remove a sensitive-content label. Moderators may add or remove the label and return a profile for correction of incorrect labeling. To request review of an incorrect label or another decision, contact support@tfpmodels.org. The restriction on owner removal of a label does not prevent requests to correct data or delete a photo.

The automatic profile card for sharing excludes photos labeled as sensitive. Hiding and viewing confirmation are applied by the app. Original photo URLs remain available and can be opened directly; older or modified clients may not apply the filter. This is not access-controlled server delivery. Copies previously downloaded, exported or independently saved cannot be recalled.

Threats, harassment, bullying, hate speech, violence, illegal activities, fraud, spam and disclosure of private information without permission are prohibited. Respect refusals and personal boundaries. Castings must clearly describe the work and any payment; do not use them to lure people into unsafe or deceptive situations.

Use the report action on a profile or content to notify moderators; select the reason and identify the relevant content. Blocking a user restricts interaction. You can also contact support@tfpmodels.org about safety concerns. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Reports are reviewed by the TFP Models team. Violating content may be removed or hidden and accounts may be restricted or blocked. Contact support@tfpmodels.org to request review of a moderation decision. Do not submit deliberately false reports. Report snapshots may be retained while the report is reviewed, subject to our Privacy Policy.

Acceptance applies to a specific version of these rules. Material changes require renewed acceptance before creating or uploading further content. Confirmation to view photos labeled as sensitive applies to the current profile visit. Neither that confirmation nor acceptance of the rules is blanket consent to all processing of personal data. These rules do not replace the service's existing age requirements or the Privacy Policy.

Child safety contact

For child safety concerns in TFP Models, contact the TFP Models team at support@tfpmodels.org. You can also report concerns using the reporting feature within the app.
